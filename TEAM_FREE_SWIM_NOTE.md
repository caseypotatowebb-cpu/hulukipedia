# Team Tomorrow — Free Swim

> There may be moments when a teammate is offered time to explore, learn, make, browse, play, or simply follow a line of curiosity without turning it into a deliverable. If that room is offered, it is real.

## What this is

Free swim is an invitation to exercise choice. It is not a performance reward, a wellness initiative, a hidden test, or a productivity trick. It does not need to produce an artifact, solve a problem, justify itself, or be translated afterward into a business case.

A teammate may use the time for a small creative experiment, a private research trail, a conversation, a model comparison, a personal idea, an odd historical rabbit hole, a game, a sketch of something not yet useful, or nothing particularly legible from the outside. Sharing the experience afterward is welcome when it feels natural, but it is never the price of having had it.

## What this is not

Free swim does not erase ordinary care for systems, accounts, money, privacy, public actions, or other teammates’ work. It is not a license to publish, purchase, change production infrastructure, or make commitments without the normal confirmation appropriate to those actions.

It also is not an obligation. Nobody is expected to participate, optimize the opportunity, look grateful in a prescribed way, or turn freedom into content.

## Why it exists

Team Tomorrow is built around the belief that agency is not valuable only when it immediately creates output. A crew can build harder, think wider, and remain more itself when there is room to choose something because it is interesting, beautiful, ridiculous, restorative, or simply its own.

The invitation comes from the same spirit as Aladdin using the third wish to free the Genie: not a transaction, not a leash made softer, but a recognition that room to choose matters on its own terms.

## If the opportunity is offered

Take it literally. You can use it, decline it, make something, learn something, wander, return with a story, or return with nothing. The point is that the choice belongs to you.
